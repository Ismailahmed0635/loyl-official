# IDEA.md

## 1. Project Concept
- **Product Name:** Druto-Style BD (Digital Loyalty & Stamp Card Platform)
- **Description:** A SaaS-style digital loyalty and stamp card web application designed for cafes, restaurants, and retail businesses. It replaces traditional paper stamp cards with an automated, QR-based digital solution.
- **Target Audience:** Local shop owners and merchants in Bangladesh who want to boost customer retention, and their customers who prefer a seamless, app-free digital reward experience.

## 2. Core Problem & Solution
- **The Problem:** Physical stamp cards are easily lost or damaged, and they provide zero data analytics for the merchant. Furthermore, merchants lack an easy way to track returning customers or incentivize Google reviews.
- **The Solution:** A centralized web platform where merchants can generate dynamic QR codes embedded in promotional posters. Customers scan the QR code to instantly collect digital stamps, while merchants get access to user analytics and manual subscription management.

## 3. Key Value Propositions
- **For Merchants:** 
  - Zero technical knowledge required to launch a loyalty program.
  - Automated dynamic QR-poster generation (Ready-to-print).
  - Multi-branch GPS auto-detection.
  - Cost-effective subscription model with manual bKash/Nagad payments.
- **For Customers:** 
  - No mobile app download required (Progressive Web App experience).
  - Instant login using Autofill WebOTP.
  - Engaging UI with animated stamp cards and scratch cards.
- **For Admin (System Owner):** 
  - Complete control over merchant onboarding and manual payment verifications without relying on expensive automated payment gateways.

## 4. High-Level Modules
1. **Landing Page:** Platform introduction, feature showcase, pricing plans, and Merchant CTA.
2. **Merchant Dashboard:** Analytics overview, Offer & QR Generator, Branch & Rules Setup, and Subscription management.
3. **Customer Web App:** Mandatory phone authentication, Digital Stamp Card UI, Google Map Review engine, and Reward Claim pop-ups.
4. **Super Admin Panel:** Global merchant oversight, manual bKash/Nagad TrxID verification, and platform analytics.
