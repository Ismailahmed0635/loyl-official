## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an ultra-clean, modern, conversion-driven visual identity tailored for modern merchant commerce across Bangladesh and global markets—spanning boutique cafes, salons, athletic clubs, and dining establishments. It merges the institutional trustworthiness of enterprise SaaS with the tactile, hospitable warmth expected by physical brick-and-mortar operators.

The visual style is **Contemporary Organic Minimalist**: 
- **Pristine surfaces & contrast:** High-contrast neutral canvases punctuated by deep heritage botanical tones convey financial dependability, operational longevity, and enterprise sophistication.
- **Merchant hospitality:** Polished, real-world terminal and mobile preview cards establish instantaneous empathy with cafe and retail managers navigating fast-paced front-of-house operations.
- **Conversion-driven clarity:** Decisive information hierarchy, clean functional cards, clear numeric proof points, and pill badges direct attention toward customer retention metrics and onboarding actions.

## Colors
The palette balances authoritative heritage greens with energetic, high-intent crimson accents across clean neutral foundations:

- **Primary (`#195035` - Dark Forest Green):** The core anchor for structural UI, primary call-to-actions, navigation bars, tier status markers, and authoritative merchant analytics.
- **Secondary (`#831b36` - Deep Maroon Red):** Reserved for high-conversion focus elements, urgent promotional triggers, high-value reward tiers, limited-time customer incentives, and key conversion badges.
- **Tertiary (`#2E7D56` - Pine Leaf Accent):** An operational mid-green for positive financial trends, successful redemption states, active customer counts, and interactive hover shifts.
- **Neutrals:**
  - **Charcoal Black (`#1A1A1A`):** Deep editorial neutral for primary headings, high-density data readouts, and core iconography.
  - **Muted Slate (`#5A605B`):** Secondary typography for merchant metadata, tooltips, and sub-labels.
  - **Warm Off-White (`#F7F7F5`):** Soft, non-glare surface base for alternate card containers, nested table backgrounds, and section banding.
  - **Pure Surface White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity foreground canvas for input groups, metric dashboards, and customer-facing simulated card displays.
  - **Border Muted (`#E5E6E1`):** Precise dividing lines ensuring clean separation without heavy visual friction.

## Typography
The system standardizes on **Inter** to ensure clinical precision, rapid visual parsing, and universal legibility across diverse hardware environments—ranging from entry-level Android retail devices to high-DPI Windows POS workstations and Retina displays.

- **Headlines:** Tightly tracked with negative letter spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.015em`) and solid weights (`600` and `700`) to create authoritative presence without sacrificing modern elegance.
- **Body Text:** Neutral tracking with standard line-heights calibrated at 150% to maximize scanability in data-dense reward catalogs, transaction logs, and customer directory tables.
- **Badges & Micro-Copy:** Scaled to uppercase tracked typography (`0.06em`) to render compact metadata, VIP tiers, and status pills crisp even on low-cost displays.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid designed for seamless conversion flows and multi-device previewing:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, max-width `1240px`, centered with `margin` of `3rem` and standard column `gutter` of `1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid with `margin` scaled to `2rem` and `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Complex dual dashboards stack into vertical stages.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `margin-mobile` of `1.25rem`, and `gutter-mobile` of `1rem`. UI blocks stack into linear thumb-friendly streams.

Spacing rhythm follows strict 4px/8px multiples (`0.25rem` to `2.5rem`) for interior component padding, separating form fields, list items, and dashboard metrics systematically.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this design system is engineered through subtle tonal layering and soft ambient diffused shadows, ensuring high rendering performance across Android WebViews and Windows Chrome instances:

- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** Base background utilizing `#FFFFFF` for hero/content sections, contrasted cleanly against `#F7F7F5` for alternating workflow blocks and comparison tables.
- **Layer 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface elevated over `#F7F7F5` with a delicate, calibrated boundary: `1px solid #E5E6E1` paired with a diffuse ambient shadow: `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(25, 80, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Layer 2 (Interactive Floating Elements & Phone Mockups):** Lifted UI cards, dropdown menus, and device chassis apply dual-stage depth: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px 0px rgba(25, 80, 53, 0.03)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Modal dialogs, QR redemption sheets, and quick-add merchant drawers sit atop a darkened scrim (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.45)`) supported by a clean shadow: `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.16)`.

## Shapes
The visual form language balances contemporary friendliness with disciplined corporate polish:

- **Base Cards & Modules:** Consistent `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, presenting clean structural geometry without feeling aggressive.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Fields:** `0.5rem` (8px) to align directly with card boundaries and maintain rhythmic consistency.
- **Pill Badges & Status Chips:** Full circular radius (`9999px`) creating an organic contrast against the underlying card grids.
- **Device Frames & Phone Mockups:** Polished `2.25rem` (36px) outer corners with a sleek `1px` structural outline (`#E5E6E1`) mimicking modern flagship hardware.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Dark Forest Green (`#195035`) fill with pure white typography, `font-weight: 600`, `padding: 0.75rem 1.5rem`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`. Smooth hover transition shifting to `#2E7D56` with micro-elevation (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / High Intent:** Deep Maroon Red (`#831b36`) fill with pure white typography for core demo requests or revenue-linked actions. Hover state transitions to `#6A152B`.
- **Outline Merchant Action:** Transparent fill, `1.5px solid #195035`, text `#195035`. Hover state fills with `rgba(25, 80, 53, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent fill, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover fills with `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)`.

### Badges & Pill Tags
- **Category & ROI Badges:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 9999px`), `padding: 0.25rem 0.75rem`, `font-size: 11px`, `text-transform: uppercase`.
- **Forest Variant:** Pale green wash (`rgba(25, 80, 53, 0.08)`) with Dark Forest Green text (`#195035`).
- **Maroon Variant:** Pale maroon wash (`rgba(131, 27, 54, 0.08)`) with Deep Maroon Red text (`#831b36`).

### Cards & Metrics Containers
- Built on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E5E6E1` and ambient shadow.
- Interior padding set to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`).
- Metric headers display bold numerical values (`28px` to `40px`) paired with a pill growth-indicator badge (+34% retention) sitting flush at the top right.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D5D6D0`, text `#1A1A1A`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, `border-radius: 0.5rem`.
- Focus state reveals a distinct non-intrusive focus ring: `border-color: #195035` and `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(25, 80, 53, 0.15)`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size `18px x 18px`, border `1.5px solid #D5D6D0`, `border-radius: 4px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio).
- Active state fills with `#195035` showcasing a crisp white checkmark or center pip.

### Interactive Device & Phone Mockups
- Polished simulated mobile screens highlighting merchant loyalty passes (QR stamps, point ladders, tiered VIP badges).
- Built with a subtle hardware frame: `border: 8px solid #1A1A1A`, `border-radius: 36px`, inside display background `#FFFFFF`. Floating micro-receipts and stamp animations break past the container edge for visual depth.
