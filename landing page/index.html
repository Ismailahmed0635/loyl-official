Loyl is a modern, QR-based digital loyalty card platform designed for storefronts, cafes, salons, and retail businesses. It replaces printed punch cards with effortless digital stamps accessible on smartphones without downloading any app.

You place your unique Loyl QR code at your checkout counter or tables. When customers scan it using their phone camera, their loyalty card opens in their mobile browser. Cashiers enter a quick 4-digit PIN or scan a receipt code to validate stamps.
