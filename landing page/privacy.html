Privacy Policy

Loyl is a QR-based digital loyalty platform for cafés, salons, gyms, restaurants and retail shops in Bangladesh. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Effective: 30 September 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026
What we collect How we use it Location Sharing Retention Your rights

1. Overview

Loyl has two kinds of users: merchants (businesses running a loyalty program) and customers (shoppers collecting stamps). Customers never need to download an app — the loyalty card opens in the phone browser after scanning the merchant's QR code.

We collect only what is needed to run the loyalty program: verify identity by phone OTP, record stamps and rewards, detect the correct branch, and show merchants aggregate analytics.

2. What we collect

Merchants: business name, owner/staff phone numbers, business address and branch locations, offer and reward settings, payment screenshots for billing verification, and support messages you send us.

Customers: full name and phone number (verified by one-time passcode), stamp and reward history, visit timestamps, and an approximate GPS snapshot at scan time used for branch detection.

Automatically: basic device and browser information, pages visited, and error logs used to keep the service reliable. We do not collect precise continuous location — only a single snapshot when you tap “scan”.

3. How we use it

  • Create and sign you in with phone OTP; keep sessions secure.
  • Record stamps, enforce one stamp per customer per shop per 24 hours, and prevent reward fraud.
  • Detect the nearest branch so stamps land on the correct store.
  • Show merchants analytics (visit frequency, peak hours, repeat rates) — always aggregated, never sold.
  • Send transactional reminders (OTP, reward ready, visit follow-ups) by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Provide support and improve reliability.

4. Location data

When a customer scans, the browser may ask for location permission. If granted, we compare the snapshot against the merchant's branch coordinates (haversine check) to auto-select the store. If denied, the scan may ask you to choose the branch manually or fail with a location error. We never track you in the background.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share the minimum necessary with:

  • Your merchant: a customer’s name, phone and stamp history are visible to the shop where they collected stamps — so staff can validate rewards.
  • Service providers: hosting, OTP/SMS delivery and error monitoring, bound by confidentiality and used only to operate Loyl.
  • Legal: if required by Bangladeshi law or to prevent fraud and abuse.

6. Retention & security

We keep account and stamp records while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards for fraud prevention and tax records, then delete or anonymise them. OTP codes expire within minutes and are never stored in readable form longer than needed.

Sessions are signed tokens stored in a secure cookie; staff PINs and device approvals are verified server-side. No system is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard safeguards and limit staff access to what is strictly needed.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Merchants may request export of their own business data. Contact us at hello@loyl.app or via the contact page (email + WhatsApp). We reply within 7 working days. Deleting a customer profile removes future stamp history; already-redeemed reward logs may be retained in anonymised form for accounting.

8. Children

Loyl is intended for general audiences and business use. Customers under 13 should use it only with a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we will post the new version here with a new effective date and, where appropriate, notify merchants by email or dashboard notice. Continued use of Loyl after the effective date means you accept the updated policy.

Questions? Email hello@loyl.app or reach us through contact.html.
