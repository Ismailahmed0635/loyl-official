Refund Policy

Start free, pay only when you are sure. Monthly plans, a 3-day free trial with no payment required, and a fair refund window if Loyl is not a fit.

Effective: 30 September 2026 · Applies to all paid plans

1. Free trial — no payment, no risk

Every paid subscription starts with a 3-day free trial. No payment, no card, no bKash charge is taken to start. If you cancel during the trial, nothing is billed and your test data is deleted on request.

3. 7-day money-back guarantee

If you paid for Starter or Growth and Loyl is not a fit, email us within 7 days of payment verification for a full refund — no questions about usage, with two exceptions:

  • QR stand already shipped: every paid plan includes one complimentary table-stand QR scanner, sent once after your first payment succeeds. If yours has already been dispatched, we deduct the stand + courier cost (currently ৳800 within Bangladesh) and refund the rest. Unshipped stands are cancelled at no cost.
  • Enterprise: refunds follow the agreed custom pricing and contact terms, not this 7-day window.
After the 7-day window, monthly fees are non-refundable, but you can cancel anytime and keep using Loyl until the end of your paid month.

4. Cancellation

  • Cancel anytime from the dashboard or by email — no lock-in, no cancellation fee.
  • After cancellation your program stays live until the end of the paid month, then pauses (customers see a “program paused” notice; no data is deleted for 90 days).
  • There is no pro-rata refund for partial months, branch closures, or seasonal pauses.

5. Duplicate / failed payments

Paid twice by mistake, or paid but the subscription was never activated? Send both transaction IDs and we refund the duplicate or unactivated payment in full, including within the 7-day window rules above.

6. How to request a refund

Email hello@loyl.app (or message us via the contact page) with:

  • Merchant / business name and registered phone number
  • Plan paid, amount, date, and transaction / screenshot reference
  • One-line reason (helps us improve; never blocks the 7-day guarantee)

We confirm within 2 working days and pay refunds to the original channel (bKash / bank) within 7–10 working days. Stand deductions, if any, are itemised in the confirmation email.

7. Questions

Unsure which plan fits? Talk to us before paying — we will recommend the cheapest plan that covers your branches, and the trial is always free.
